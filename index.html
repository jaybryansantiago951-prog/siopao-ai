From kitchen to table

Let’s make siopao.

0 / 5 done

  1. Mix and knead

    Stir flour, sugar, yeast, and salt in a large bowl. Add warm water and oil, then knead until smooth and springy, about 8 minutes.

    8 min hands-on

  2. Cook the asado

    Brown the pork with onion and garlic. Add soy, oyster sauce, and sugar. Simmer until tender, then stir in cornstarch slurry until glossy. Cool completely.

    25 min

  3. Fill and fold

    Divide dough into 12 pieces and roll each into a round. Add a spoonful of cooled filling, pleat the edges together, and pinch to seal.

    20 min hands-on

  4. Let them rise

    Set each bun on a small square of parchment. Cover loosely and leave in a warm spot until puffy, about 30 minutes.

    30 min resting

  5. Steam and share

    Steam over simmering water for 15–18 minutes. Turn off the heat and rest for 3 minutes before opening the lid. Best served warm.

    18 min
✳

A note from our kitchen

Good things take a little time.

Keep the steamer covered while the buns cook, and resist peeking. That steady steam is what gives siopao its lovely, cloud-soft top.
